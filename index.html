Portfolio Investment Machine

Twenty stocks in the five sectors chosen by the macro regime model, each valued with a ten-year cash-flow model. Current regime: inflation rising.

How the figures are built. Weights follow the tier rule inside each sector's weight (High = 3 units, Mid = 2, Low = 1) and are provisional. Portfolio and sector multiples are weight-averaged as harmonic means (the average of earnings yields), excluding stocks where a multiple is not meaningful: Insmed (loss-making), Neurocrine's EV/EBITDA (not retrieved) and Centrus' EV/EBITDA (230 times, on near-zero profit). Expected return is each stock's base-case return per year at today's price, from Step 4, weighted.

Sources. Multiples and market values from stockanalysis.com statistics (7 Oct 2026); Microsoft and Alphabet EV/EBITDA, price-to-sales and dividend yield from tickflow.io (8 Oct 2026); Veeva's multiples calculated from its Q2 FY27 results. S&P 500 forward P/E of about 21.5 from Zacks (June 2026). Values, returns and tiers from the project's Steps 4 to 8. This is a student project, not investment advice.
